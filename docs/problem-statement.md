# Problem Statement

## Background

Cybersecurity threats are becoming increasingly common as people and organizations depend on the internet for communication, education,business, and daily activities. Threats such as phishing, malicious links, suspicious files, malware, and unauthorized access can affect users who may not have advanced cybersecurity knowledge.Many users find it difficult to determine whether an online activity, link, or file is safe. Attackers take advantage of this lack of awareness to trick users into revealing sensitive information or installing malicious software.

## The Problem
The main problem is that users often cannot easily identify and
understand potential cyber threats before interacting with them.

Existing security warnings may provide technical information without
clearly explaining the actual risk to a normal user. As a result, users
may click suspicious links, open unsafe files, or continue risky
activities without understanding the consequences.

There is a need for a simple and intelligent solution that can analyze
potential threats and clearly explain the level of risk and the
recommended action to the user.

## Who is Affected

The problem affects:

- Students and individuals using the internet regularly
- People with limited cybersecurity knowledge
- Small businesses and organizations
- Employees accessing emails, websites, and online services
- Anyone who may encounter phishing links, suspicious files, or
  malicious online activity

## Why It Matters

Cyber attacks can lead to stolen personal information, compromised
accounts, financial loss, privacy violations, data loss, and disruption
of normal activities.

The problem is especially important because a single unsafe action,
such as clicking a malicious link or opening a suspicious file, can
give attackers an opportunity to compromise a user's device or
accounts.

Helping users recognize threats before they take action can reduce
the possibility of successful cyber attacks and improve overall
cybersecurity awareness.

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Traditional antivirus and security tools are useful for detecting many
known threats, but they may not always provide simple explanations that
normal users can understand.

Users may also depend on manually checking links, files, or suspicious
messages, which can be time-consuming and unreliable for people without
technical cybersecurity expertise.

Therefore, there is a need for an easy-to-use solution that combines
threat detection with clear explanations and actionable
recommendations, allowing users to make safer decisions.
